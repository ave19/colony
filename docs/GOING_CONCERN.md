# Going concern — player & sim contract

## World

- Simulation time is **real time 1:1** (one Earth second = one sim second) while a room is active.
- Long work (transit, fab jobs, multi-month surveys) advances via the **event queue / Warp**.
- Orbits use Keplerian periods; outer giants take years (you do not wait that out in real time).
- Humanity already observes nearby stars; dossiers exist before you open them.

## Arrival

- Ark: ~10k people, seed materials, fab capacity, full know-how.
- **Not** pre-packed survey sats, miners, or haulers.
- Colony leadership **authorizes builds**; jobs spend stock and take months.

## Agency

- Map dominates (3D orbit/zoom/focus; moons visible when focused).
- Apply unit capabilities to selected bodies / mine sites.
- Survey is continuous on-station: detail grows until **mine sites** appear; mining targets sites only.

## Loop

Open archive → commit → transit → arrive materials-only → build → order → survey → sites → industry.
